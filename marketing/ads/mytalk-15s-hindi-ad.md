# MyTalk — Copy & Paste Video Prompt (15s)

**How to use:** Open ChatGPT (or Sora / any AI video tool) → copy ONE full block below → paste → generate.  
No CapCut. No editor. Everything is inside the prompt (girl, logo, captions, timing).

---

## BLOCK 1 — Main prompt (copy everything inside the box)

```
Make a 15-second vertical video ad (9:16, 1080x1920) for MyTalk (mytalk.in).

CAST:
- Show one young Indian girl (age about 22–28), natural look, realistic skin, modern Indian city vibe.
- She should feel lonely at first, then calm and relieved — not crying, not dramatic acting.
- No hospital, no therapy couch, no doctor.

STORY (exact timing):

0–3 seconds:
- Indian girl alone at night in a small bedroom / hostel room.
- Phone face-down on the bed. She stares at the ceiling.
- Soft warm lamp light. Slow camera push-in.
- BIG Hindi caption on screen (center):
  "Sabke paas time hai…"

3–7 seconds:
- Same Indian girl sits up, puts on headphones, face softens.
- Caption changes to:
  "sunne ka nahi."
- Soft Hindi female voiceover says:
  "Sabke paas time hai… sunne ka nahi."

7–11 seconds:
- Cut to her looking at phone.
- Caption:
  "MyTalk pe private baat"
- Voiceover:
  "MyTalk pe private baat — jab dil bhari ho. Apna advisor. Apna time. Bina judgment."

11–13 seconds:
- Girl gives a small peaceful smile / soft exhale.
- Caption:
  "Bina judgment."

13–15 seconds — LOGO END CARD (very important):
- Full screen calm end card.
- SHOW THE MYTALK LOGO clearly in the center (wordmark: MyTalk, premium, deep teal + charcoal).
- Under logo, show text:
  Someone to Talk. Someone to Understand.
- At bottom, big text:
  mytalk.in
- Caption / CTA on screen:
  "Ab baat karo → mytalk.in"
- Voiceover last line:
  "Ab baat karo. MyTalk.in"

CAPTIONS RULES (must follow):
- Always show Hindi captions on screen for the full 15 seconds (for muted viewers).
- Captions must be large, white or soft cream, with dark soft shadow so readable.
- Keep captions in the safe center area (not too close to top or bottom).
- Sync captions with the voiceover lines.

VOICEOVER (exact Hindi, calm soft female voice):
"Sabke paas time hai… sunne ka nahi. MyTalk pe private baat — jab dil bhari ho. Apna advisor. Apna time. Bina judgment. Ab baat karo. MyTalk.in"

MUSIC:
- Soft emotional ambient / light piano under the voice.
- Music quieter than voiceover.
- Small gentle swell when logo appears at the end.

STYLE:
- Premium, warm, intimate, clean.
- Photoreal cinematic.
- No purple neon, no meme style, no cartoon, no watermark, no extra logos.
- Brand colors: deep forest teal and soft cream.

OUTPUT:
One finished 15-second video with:
1) Indian girl story
2) Hindi voiceover
3) Hindi captions always on screen
4) MyTalk logo on the last 2 seconds end card with mytalk.in
```
