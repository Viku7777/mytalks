About MyTalk

Someone to talk. Someone to understand. Your safe space on mytalk.in.

Our Story

MyTalk began with a simple belief: because everyone needs someone to talk to. People deserve a private place to be heard without judgment, waitlists, or social pressure—so we built a calm space where trusted listeners help you talk through what feels heavy.

Mission

Give everyone a safe space to talk freely, feel better, and find real support—through chat, voice, and video at mytalk.in.

Vision

A world where you're never alone when you need to start talking—and asking for support feels normal, safe, and stigma-free.

Why We Started

Too many people wait alone—unsure if their feelings are “serious enough,” or afraid of being judged. MyTalk exists for the in-between moments: relationships, loneliness, stress, and life decisions that still deserve care.

Social Impact

We partner with organizations to expand access to emotional support, train community listeners, and promote healthier conversations about mental well-being—always with clear limits that we are not an emergency or medical service.
