Cancellation Policy

How cancellations, late changes, and no-shows are handled.

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Customer Cancellation

Cancel at least 12 hours before the session for a full refund or free reschedule (unless a promotional term states otherwise).

Advisor Cancellation

If an advisor cancels, you may rebook with the same or another advisor at no extra cost, or request a full refund.

Late Cancellation

Cancellations inside 12 hours may incur up to 50% of the session fee.

No Show Policy

Missing a session without notice may forfeit the session fee.

Rescheduling

You may reschedule once per booking free of charge if done outside the late window and subject to advisor availability.

Contact

Questions? Email legal@mytalk.in or visit our Contact page.
