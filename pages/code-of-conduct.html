Code of Conduct

Expectations for users, advisors, and partners.

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Professional Behavior

Communicate courteously and keep sessions focused on supportive conversation.

Respect

Honor boundaries. Do not pressure anyone for personal contact outside the platform.

Confidentiality

Do not record, screenshot, or share private session content without explicit consent and legal right.

Safety

Follow the Safety Policy. Report abuse promptly.

Honesty

Provide accurate profile and booking information.

Integrity

Do not manipulate ratings, referrals, or payments.

Questions? Email legal@mytalk.in or visit our Contact page.
