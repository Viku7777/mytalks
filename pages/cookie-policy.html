How cookies and similar technologies work on MyTalk.

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Types of Cookies

Essential: required for security, theme preference, and core booking flows.

Analytics: help us understand traffic and improve pages.

Marketing: may measure campaigns if you consent.

Preferences: remember choices such as language or UI settings.

Managing Cookies

Use the cookie banner choices and your browser settings to accept, decline, or clear cookies. Declining optional cookies may limit personalization.
