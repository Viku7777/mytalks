Emergency Notice

Please read this carefully if you or someone else may be in danger.

Get help immediately

If you are in immediate danger or considering self-harm, please contact your local emergency services or a trusted crisis helpline immediately.

This platform is not designed for emergency intervention.

What to do

  1. Call your local emergency number (for example, 112 / 911 depending on your country).
  2. Contact a licensed crisis helpline available in your region.
  3. Reach out to a trusted person nearby if it is safe to do so.

What MyTalk is for

MyTalk provides non-emergency emotional support, relationship guidance, and confidential conversations with listeners and coaches. It is not a substitute for emergency response or clinical care.
