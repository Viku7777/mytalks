Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Data Collection

We collect account details (name, email, phone), booking information, limited payment metadata, device/browser data, and communications you send through support forms.

Cookies

We use necessary cookies for session preferences and optional analytics/marketing cookies with consent. See our Cookie Policy for details.

Payment Information

Card and UPI payments are processed by payment gateways. MyTalks does not store full card numbers on our servers in this demo architecture.

Security

We design for HTTPS, access controls, and least-privilege internal access. No method of transmission is perfectly secure; please use strong passwords.

Third-Party Services

We may use hosting, analytics, email, and payment providers bound by contractual confidentiality and privacy obligations.

User Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may request access, correction, deletion, or export of personal data, and withdraw optional consent.

Data Retention

We retain account and booking records as needed for service delivery, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and safety investigations, then delete or anonymize when no longer required.

Contact

Questions? Email legal@mytalknow.com or visit our Contact page.
