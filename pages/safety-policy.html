Safety Policy

How we design for safer conversations.

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Anonymous Platform

Share only what you choose. Display names may differ from legal names used for account verification.

Secure Communication

Sessions are intended to occur on MyTalks channels—not unmonitored personal apps for first contact.

Privacy Protection

We limit staff access to conversation content except for safety, legal, or abuse investigations.

Report Abuse

Use in-product reporting (when available) or Support tickets to report harmful behavior.

Block Users

Blocking tools prevent further contact where available; contact Support if you need escalation.

Advisor Verification

Advisors undergo identity and policy verification before listing. Verification does not equal clinical licensure.

Contact

Questions? Email legal@mytalk.in or visit our Contact page.
